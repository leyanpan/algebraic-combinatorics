## INFORMAL STATEMENT
def.fps.lim.isSummable

\leanhelper  A family $(f_n)_{n \in \mathbb {N}}$ of FPSs is \emph{summable} if for each $n \in \mathbb {N}$, the set $\{ i \in \mathbb {N} : [x^n] f_i \neq 0\} $ is finite.
